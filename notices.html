Cookies, refunds and other notices

Cookies. Ayee is a native iOS app. It has no website forms and sets no cookies, so it needs no cookie banner. If a marketing website is added later, it must ship with a cookie policy and a consent banner before any non-essential cookie or analytics script runs.

Refunds. The app is free with no in-app purchases, so there is nothing to refund. Any future purchase goes through Apple, whose refund process applies.

Emails. Ayee sends no marketing email. If that changes, every email needs a working one-tap unsubscribe link and a physical postal address (CAN-SPAM).

Fonts and images. The app uses Apple's system fonts (SF Pro, SF Mono, Snell Roundhand) and SF Symbols under Apple's licence for apps on Apple platforms. All illustrations (hills, sun, wheel, ghost silhouette, the Ayee face icon) are drawn in code for this app. Marketing images of the "ayee" wordmark use Inter (SIL Open Font License 1.1). No stock photos.

Third-party SDKs. One: supabase-swift (MIT licence). No analytics, ads, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
