Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Ayee ("we", "the app") is operated by Encore Acquisition LLC, contact encoreacquisition19@gmail.com. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, no data sales.
  • You don't give us an email, phone number or real name. The app creates a private guest account for you.
  • What you post in a pact is visible only to people in that pact.
  • Delete your account any time in Profile → Delete account. It's immediate and permanent.

What stays on your phone

  • Screen Time app choices (Glow-up app lock): Apple gives the app opaque tokens, not app names. They never leave your device.
  • Camera frames for face alignment are processed on-device by Apple's Vision framework. A photo is uploaded only when you take a proof photo for a pact.
  • Glow-up streaks and local tab are stored on-device only.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally. We don't use push notification servers.

Service providers

We use Supabase (Supabase Inc., servers in the United States, region us-west-1) to host the database, sign-in and photo storage. Supabase processes data only on our behalf. We use no other third-party SDKs.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account or leave a pact. When a pact you own still has other members, ownership passes to the longest-standing member, and your own entries are removed when you delete your account.

Your rights

You can see and edit your data in the app. You can delete everything with Profile → Delete account: this removes your profile, check-ins, proof photos, comments, votes, call-outs, IOUs and feed posts about you. If you can't open the app, email encoreacquisition19@gmail.com from any address with your display name and pact code, and we'll delete the account within 30 days. Residents of the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA) have rights to access, correct, delete and port data; we honor them for everyone. We don't sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Age

Ayee is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the app, email us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our host. Database rules ensure only pact members can read a pact's data. Proof photos sit in a private bucket and are shared through links that expire after an hour.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll show a notice in the app before it takes effect.
