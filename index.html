Common questions

How do I join a friend's pact? Tap Join with a code on the Pacts tab and enter the 6-character code they shared.

Who can see my check-ins and photos? Only the people in that pact.

Does Ayee move money? No. Cash stakes are IOUs you settle with friends however you like. Ayee never holds, sends or charges money.

How do I report or block someone? Tap ••• on a post, or long-press a comment, then choose Report or Block.

How do I delete my account? Open Profile → Delete account. It's immediate and permanent. If you can't open the app, email us with your display name and pact code and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Why does the app lock other apps? Only if you turn on the Glow-up app lock. It uses Apple's Screen Time and you can turn it off any time in Profile.
